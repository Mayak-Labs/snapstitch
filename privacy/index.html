Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

This policy explains how Snapstitch Pro (“the app”), made by Mayak Labs (“we”, “us”), handles your information. In short: your photos stay on your device, there is no account, and we don't track you or show ads.

Your photos

The app reads only the photos you choose, either in the app or through the share sheet. Stitching, annotating and framing happen entirely on your iPhone. Stitched images are saved in the app's own storage on your device, and to your photo library only when you ask. We never upload your photos and we can't see them.

Purchases

Snapstitch Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription history, and basic technical data such as your device's IP address, iOS version, app version and region. This is used only to provide and restore your subscription.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No advertising and no third-party analytics
  • No notifications
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • We don't sell or share your personal information

Data retention and deletion

Everything the app creates is stored on your device. Deleting a Snap in the app, or deleting the app, removes it. To ask us to delete the subscription records RevenueCat holds for your anonymous app user ID, email us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.

Common questions

How do I stitch screenshots?

Open the Stitch tab, pick your screenshots in order and tap Stitch. You can also select screenshots in Photos, tap Share and choose Snapstitch.

Snapstitch doesn't show up in the share sheet

In the share sheet, scroll the app row to the end, tap More, and turn on Snapstitch. The extension appears when you share images.

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Snapstitch Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
